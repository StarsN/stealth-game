Quiet Hours — a top-down stealth game. Night shift at the Halcyon Museum: three artifacts are misfiled in the vault wing. Slip past the patrolling guards, pocket all three, and reach the green exit unseen. Guards sweep the rooms with vision cones that bend around walls and display cases; run and they will hear you, sneak and you are harder to spot, and once the meter fills the chase is on.

## Run it
